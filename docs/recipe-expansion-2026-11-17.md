## Content decisions

- Native Celsius settings stay at or below200°C; Fahrenheit at or below400°F. The other-scale list is discovery only,not a converted clock.
- Native Celsius and Fahrenheit versions of a familiar ingredient remain independent methods. Count115means published recipe subjects,not115unique ingredients or115methods in either preset.
- The Japanese hard rice-cake method is labelled kirimochi; Ningbo rice cakes,filled mochi and tangyuan are not substitutes. Frozen Kirkland tempura shrimp is explicitly a fully cooked product;wet-battered raw shrimp is not supported.
- The cauliflower method needs a hob and a5-minute boil before air frying;this dependency appears before cooking. Butter that must be melted is stated with required equipment.
- Reference quantities expressed as counts remain counts;missing piece weights are not fabricated. Editorial seasoning starting amounts are identified for lotus,chives,squid and potato crisps.
- Keep missing candidates outside the public library when only “maximum heat”,unknown starting state,oven-only or steam-specific instructions can be found. Frozen raw chicken wings are still not a Celsius direct-start method.
- Review caught an ambiguous corn-flour name in the okra reference;the published method uses its explicitly permitted breadcrumb alternative. A copied internet picture/unknown shrimp-paste packet was not used as an independent exact-portion reference.

## Search behavior

- Bounded cooking questions:空气炸锅烤藕片多久,氣炸鍋杏鮑菇做法,how long to air fry frozen wings,salmon in an air fryer.
- Full pinyin,with or without spaces:you tiao,jichi,xingbaogu,wu hua rou. No noisy one-letter initials.
- Everyday aliases and traditional variants:西蓝花/西兰花,花菜/菜花,莲藕/藕片,菠萝/凤梨.
- Exact/phrase/Chinese-subsequence ingredient matches take precedence over spelling repair. The real-catalog test found frozen wings could otherwise surface frozen onion rings after filtering out thawed wings;it now returns no direct method and explicitly prepared alternatives.
- Unmatched raw/cooked qualifiers and negations remain unresolved. Unknown foods do not become a fabricated recipe. Ready-to-bake queries also match the separate dough/batter state.
- Saved-preset filtering,category recovery,URL restoration,IME handling and query-free analytics remain in the existing search flow.
