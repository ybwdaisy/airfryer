# Independent audit: food batch B

Reviewed 2026-11-12 by the batch-A researcher, who did not author batch B. Read the actual primary pages for chicken nuggets, fish sticks, onion rings, biscuits, cinnamon rolls, cookies, pizza and grilled cheese, plus selected independent cross-checks. No source data was edited in this review.

## Findings to fix

1. **Cinnamon rolls: preserve the liner-removal step (P2).** The displayed temperature and 10–12-minute total match Pillsbury, but the stored instructions reduce the halfway handling to “turn the rolls.” The original method inverts the rolls onto a plate after six minutes, **discards the parchment**, and returns them topping-side up before the remaining 4–6 minutes. Keeping the liner for the second stage changes the referenced method and can change underside cooking. Update `turn`, `steps`, and the portion wording so the liner is explicitly used only for the first stage. The missing steps are in the publicly returned page HTML even though the web text extraction omits steps 3–4. Source: [Pillsbury original cinnamon-roll section](https://www.pillsbury.com/everyday-eats/we-tried-these-products-in-an-air-fryer-and-heres-what-actually-worked).

2. **Cookies: retain the full cooling/setting instructions (P2).** The reference’s 300°F / 13–14 minutes and five small flattened dough rounds are correct. It then specifies five minutes in the basket followed by **15 more minutes on the foil on a cooling rack before removing the cookies**. The current text mentions only the five-minute basket rest, which makes the abbreviated method sound complete too early and risks the still-soft centers falling apart. Add the additional on-foil cooling stage to `steps` or `notes`; do not change the cooking interval. Source: [Giant Food / Pillsbury recipe, step 3](https://recipecenter.giantfood.com/recipes/242362/pillsbury-air-fryer-caramel-chocolate-chip-cookies).

## Checks without a mismatch

- **Chicken nuggets:** Tyson explicitly identifies the named product as fully cooked and frozen and gives 390°F / 7 minutes. The entry correctly excludes raw breaded products; no unprovided batch weight or preheat rule was invented. [Manufacturer page](https://www.tyson.com/products/nuggets-patties/00023700061874).
- **Fish sticks:** Gorton’s confirms uncooked/frozen, one layer, 400°F / 7–9 minutes, and a manufacturer-specific 165°F center. The `safetyOverride` is necessary and correct. [Manufacturer directions](https://www.gortons.com/products/crunchy-breaded-fish-sticks/).
- **Onion rings:** Retailer-reproduced Alexia directions confirm preheat 360°F, basket at most half full, 12–14 minutes and a halfway shake. The entry correctly does not treat the 13.5 oz pack size as the mandatory basket quantity. [Package directions via Harris Teeter](https://www.harristeeter.com/p/alexia-crispy-onion-rings-frozen-onion-rings/0083418300705).
- **Biscuits:** Pillsbury confirms four refrigerated Grands Flaky Layers biscuits, no preheat, 320°F, six minutes then a turn and five to seven more. The cross-check correctly cites EFC’s *canned* subsection rather than its frozen-biscuit recipe. [Pillsbury](https://www.pillsbury.com/everyday-eats/we-tried-these-products-in-an-air-fryer-and-heres-what-actually-worked), [EFC canned subsection](https://www.everydayfamilycooking.com/air-fryer-biscuits/).
- **Pizza:** EFC confirms cold leftover slices in one layer, preheat 350°F, 3–4 minutes with additional checking if needed. The separate official 165°F leftovers requirement must remain visible; the recipe’s melted-cheese description alone is insufficient. [Recipe](https://www.everydayfamilycooking.com/reheat-pizza-in-air-fryer/).
- **Grilled cheese:** Love & Lemons confirms one fresh cheese sandwich, preheat 375°F, 3–5 minutes then 2–5 after turning. The entry’s `state: fresh` is correct; “Prepared & reheat” category wording in the app avoids implying that this is a leftover-reheating instruction. [Recipe](https://www.loveandlemons.com/air-fryer-grilled-cheese/).
- **Cookie cross-check:** Food Lovin Family’s body says 350°F / 5–6 minutes while its recipe card says 320°F / 5 minutes. The dataset accurately attributes its evidence to the card and discloses differing preparation. It should stay a contextual cross-check, not become an adjustment rule. [Source](https://www.foodlovinfamily.com/how-to-cook-refrigerated-cookie-dough-in-air-fryer/).

This is a source-consistency review, not an actual cooking test. No numerical baseline mismatch was found in the eight sampled records.
