# Research review, group B

Reviewed 2026-11-12. `foods-b.json` contains 25 foods and 27 reference variants. All variants retain two independently authored sources. Sources were read; numerical extraction from the Cook Smarts PDF was checked with layout-preserving PDF text because flattened web extraction mismatched several rows. Entries paraphrase the preparation and retain links rather than reproducing complete recipe text.

## Boundaries that must stay visible

- The second source is a cross-check, not proof that two sources agree on an identical setting. Different times and preparations are retained in evidence and notes; no temperatures or times were averaged.
- Fish sticks use the Gorton's **uncooked** product instructions. Its required center temperature is **165°F**, not the general fresh-fish 145°F. The variant has a `safetyOverride` with the manufacturer link.
- Chicken nuggets use a specifically named **fully cooked** Tyson Game Time product. The manufacturer supports 390°F for seven minutes. Raw breaded nuggets are excluded. Air-fryer batch quantity and preheat are not stated by this label and are not invented.
- Dumplings are restricted to the recipe's vegetable-potsticker option. Raw meat, thick dumplings and products that require steaming are not equivalent. Spring rolls are similarly restricted to the vegetable version.
- Onion rings follow Alexia package directions reproduced by Harris Teeter: preheat 360°F, basket no more than half full, 12–14 minutes. An earlier independent recipe's four-minute small batch was deliberately not selected because current package data is more authoritative. Jalapeño poppers similarly follow Farm Rich's own 390°F/eight-minute article, with an independent Farm Rich recipe cross-check.
- Baking guides are product-specific. Biscuits and cinnamon rolls use the Pillsbury cold-start method; cookies use the Pillsbury-sponsored small 24-count-package rounds on foil in a 3.7-quart basket. Different dough weights, raw homemade dough and frozen dough are excluded.
- The cookie reference is a caramel-cookie recipe, but the displayed 13–14-minute stage is before adding the candy. No topping change is used to recalculate baking time.
- Grilled cheese is **fresh preparation**, not reheating, despite its navigation category. Pizza is reheating; its clock is an initial check and not a substitute for reaching the leftovers safety target.
- Potatoes and fries have two supported preparation choices. Neither creates a generic weight/thickness conversion rule.

## Sources deliberately not used

- The old Ninja UK attachment at `https://support.ninjakitchen.co.uk/hc/article_attachments/13351901593756` currently redirects to the brand homepage; it was not retained as supporting evidence.
- Generic competitor lookup sites and unattributed generated-looking charts were not used.
- Search-volume rankings and physical cooking validation were not performed and must not be claimed.

No assigned food was silently replaced. Further arbitrary weights, raw-meat frozen-product substitutions and extrapolated frozen/room-temperature variants remain unsupported.
