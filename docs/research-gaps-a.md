# Research notes: food batch A

25 distinct entries, 28 public variants, each with two independently authored sources actually opened on 2026-11-12. The default reference sets the displayed values; cross-checks are not averaged.

- COSORI’s current chart is specific to the Iconic and frequently differs in appliance setting, food shape, or size. These differences are disclosed per entry. No generic multiplier was adopted.
- Bell peppers were strengthened from a sparse chart entry to Simply Air Fryer’s recipe: 3 peppers in roughly ½-inch strips, 380°F, 10–13 minutes, shake every 4 minutes. The independent COSORI cross-check uses dice and is disclosed as a different preparation.
- Turkey breast uses a fully thawed 3 lb boneless, skin-on roast; it is not a whole-turkey entry. The primary recipe suggests Roast if available.
- Several culinary sources describe removing pork or fish early and relying on carryover, or offering rare beef/shellfish. Those stopping rules are NOT carried into site instructions. Official safety guidance must remain separate and override estimates.
- Meatballs are explicitly lean beef only despite the primary recipe also permitting poultry. It uses a conservative 165°F finish; do not add poultry as an alias without a separate safety mapping.
- Bacon has no generic safety identifier because its thin cured slices do not fit a whole-cut meat instruction cleanly. The entry directs users to package safe-cooking instructions; parent implementation should retain that note.
- Chicken-breast size ranges are transcribed from Skinnytaste’s size chart at its 380°F setting, including the source’s pounded/refrigerated-brined preparation. The large chart band is open-ended; the note explicitly forbids arbitrary size extrapolation.
- Individual piece weights are absent in the chicken-thigh and sausage references and are not invented.
- No real-device cooking acceptance was performed, and these records do not claim recipe testing by this project.
